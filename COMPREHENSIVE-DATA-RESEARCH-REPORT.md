## === CURRENT ARTIFACT SYSTEM ===

### Temel Yapı (Doğrulanmış — launch'tan Nisan 2026'ya kadar)

**Kaynak:** game8, blueberries.gg, vg247, Reddit, dotesports

- **4 slot** her karakter için
- **Slot 1-2:** Defensive attributes (Health Boost, Shield Boost, Damage Resistance, vb.)
- **Slot 3-4:** Offensive attributes (Weapon Enhancement, Elemental Boosts, vb.)
- **Rarity:** Mythic, Exotic (Exotic'ler element-locked)
- **Set bonus YOK** (vg247: "don't come in sets the same way Genshin Impact's artifacts do")

### Artifact Özellikleri

Her artifact:
- **Power:** Toplam güç değeri
- **Gear Level:** Artifact seviyesi (max 85 — Aralık 2025 güncellemesi)
- **Attributes:** Random stat'lar (Health Boost, Shield Boost, Weapon Damage, vb.)
- **Set Effect:** Artifact'in benzersiz efekti (her artifact'in kendine özel)
- **Star Tier:** 1-5 (Attribute multiplier'ları artırır)

### Artifact Set Effect Örnekleri (Doğrulanmış)

**Slot 3:**
- Courageous Planetesimal: Ability → +18% damage for 10s
- Bellicose Precession: Rapid-Fire → +37% rate of fire after 5 hits
- Calming Token: Piercing → +10 accuracy, +46% precision damage (stationary)
- Jolted Transit: Impact → -20% precision, +37% body shot damage
- Ring of Courage: Overshield → -10% damage taken, +20% damage while shielded

**Slot 4:**
- Valiant Veil: Move → +25% damage for 5s after movement ability

- Rampaging Precession: Rapid-Fire → +40% damage after 10+ hits then reload

## === CURRENT WEAPON SYSTEM ===

### Weapon Upgrade Systems (Doğrulanmış)

**Kaynak:** Reddit upgrading guide (Apr 2026), game8, Sportskeeda (Mar 2026)

#### 1. Gear Level
- Max 80 (standart)
- 80+ sadece metastable cores ile (Grandmaster activities)
- Rare silahlar gear level upgrade YAPILAMAZ
- Legendary+ silahlar gear level upgrade yapabilir

#### 2. Enhancement (+0 to +10)
- Enhancement Prisms (combat style specific: Rapid-Fire, Piercing, Spread, Impact)
- Infusion Cores (level 8-10 için)
- Lumenite (level +10 için)
- Enhancement aynı isimli tüm silah kopyalarında paylaşılır

#### 3. Intrinsic Trait
- Silahın frame'ine bağlı sabit özellik
- Değiştirilemez
- Örnek: "Lightweight Frame" (Wolfpack SMG), "Aggressive Frame" (DEL5 Kulfi)

#### 4. Origin Trait
- Foundry'ye (manufacturer) bağlı sabit özellik
- Değiştirilemez
- Örnek: "Paranoia" (Heron foundry)

#### 5. Random Perks (4 perks, 3 categories)
- Perk 1 slot
- Perk 2 slot (Perk 1 ile aynı perk gelemez)
- Perk 3 slot
- Perk rarity: Rare (300 power), Legendary (350 power), Mythic (800 power)
- Legendary silahlar: Rare + Legendary perk roll
- Mythic silahlar: Rare + Legendary + Mythic perk roll

#### 6. Weapon Mods
- Scope, Magazine, Ammo modları
- Combat style specific
- Rare/Legendary/Mythic/Exotic mod rarity
- Perk level enhancement (1-5)
- Stats: Damage, Range, Handling, Reload Speed
- Exotic silahlar mod YOK, Catalyst var

#### 7. Weapon Forging (Season 4 — Mart 2026)
- Mythic silahlar için perk upgrade sistemi
- Duplicate silah kullanarak perk upgrade
- "Uncommon Mythic" silahlar: Enhanced perk versiyonları
- Mythic Weapon Parameter malzeme gerektirir

#### 8. Exotic Catalysts
- Exotic silahlar için mod yerine Catalyst
- Silahın perk'lerini geliştirir

#### 9. Exotic Resonance (Haziran 2026)
- Resmi patch notes'ta bahsediliyor
- "Vibro-Frequency Grains", "Battle-Refined Crystal", "Protocol Crystal" materyalleri
- Ayrıntılı mekanik doğrulanamadı
