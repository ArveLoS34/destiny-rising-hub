## === CURRENT WEAPON SYSTEM ===

### Tam Weapon Upgrade Sistemi (Doğrulanmış)

```
WEAPON SYSTEM
├── Gear Level (1-80, 80+ metastable cores)
├── Enhancement (+0 to +10)
│   ├── Enhancement Prisms (combat style specific)
│   ├── Infusion Cores (+8 to +10)
│   └── Lumenite (+10)
├── Intrinsic Trait (sabit, frame-based)
├── Origin Trait (sabit, foundry-based)
├── Random Perks (4 perk, 3 category)
│   ├── Perk Slot 1
│   ├── Perk Slot 2 (Slot 1 ile aynı perk gelemez)
│   └── Perk Slot 3
├── Weapon Mods (Rare/Legendary/Mythic/Exotic)
│   ├── Scope mod
│   ├── Magazine mod
│   └── Ammo mod
│   └── Perk Level Enhancement (1-5)
├── Adept Mods (S5+)
├── Forging (S4+)
│   ├── Perk upgrade (duplicate silah ile)
│   └── Uncommon Mythic → Enhanced perk transfer
├── Catalysts (Exotic silahlar)
└── Exotic Resonance (S5+, Haziran 2026)
    ├── Harmonic Resonance (Main Attr + random attrs)
    ├── Mastery Resonance (Main Attr + random attrs)
    └── Protocol Resonance (weapon-exclusive effect)
        ├── Harmonic Rune (re-roll random attrs)
        └── Anchor Rune (lock attr)
```
